# Citcon
> Global payments platform enabling merchants to accept local wallets,
> QR, and mobile payments across 100+ countries.
 
Citcon provides unified payment infrastructure connecting merchants with
local payment methods including digital wallets, QR code systems, and
bank transfers. Our platform simplifies cross-border payments, improves
authorization rates, and helps global merchants localize checkout.
